    How SignalSense decides, step by step

    1 · Sense in PCU

    Each approach has a camera region of interest. Detections are weighted by passenger-car units, so a bus is not counted like a scooter.

    D = Σ PCUqueued + 0.6 Σ PCUmoving + priority
    Two-wheeler0.5Car1.0Auto-rickshaw1.2Bus, truck3.0

    2 · Plan with Webster

    Arrivals from the last 90 s give each phase a flow ratio y. Webster's formula gives the cycle and the green split.

    C₀ = (1.5L + 5) / (1 − Y)
    gᵢ = (C₀ − L) · yᵢ / Y

    3 · Decide by pressure

    Every half-second the engine compares pressure on both phases. A crowded downstream link counts against sending more traffic into it.

    min 7 s · max 45 s green · max 70 s red

    Switches on gap-out, a met split, or strong imbalance.

    4 · Green corridor

    GPS reports the ambulance 7 s upstream. Each junction works out how early it must turn green so the queue ahead has time to drain.

    lead = transition + 2 s + queue × 1.2 s

    Arms when ETA ≤ lead + 4 s. Releases once the vehicle clears the box.

    5 · Stay safe

    Every change runs 3 s amber and 2 s all-red. A phase is cut only after a 3 s minimum. If a camera drops out, that junction falls back to its fixed plan while the GPS corridor keeps working.

    AI recommends, controller enforces

    This is a simulation model, not field data. Traffic rates, the vehicle mix, PCU factors (indicative, after IRC:106) and calibration constants are illustrative. Detection boxes stand in for YOLO output. In the prototype the counts come from a YOLOv8 model trained on Indian traffic classes.
