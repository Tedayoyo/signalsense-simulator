    What is simulated

    Detection. The page stands in for YOLOv8 by reading which vehicles sit inside each 55 m camera zone, then draws the boxes. Everything after that runs for real on that input: PCU scoring, green allocation, the starvation guard, preemption and the fail-safe.

    The baseline

    60 s cycle, 26 s green each way, 3 s yellow, 1 s all-red, no coordination between junctions. Priority vehicles obey the signals and cannot pass a full queue. Traffic keeps left. PCU weights: two-wheeler 0.5, car 1.0, auto-rickshaw 1.2, bus 2.2 (IRC 106-1990).

    Safety rules that never bend

    7 s minimum green, 3 s yellow and 1 s all-red on every change, including preemption. No approach waits past 60 s while it has demand. If a camera feed drops, that junction falls back to the fixed plan and still honours V2X alerts. Idle fuel assumes 0.6 L/h per stopped PCU.
