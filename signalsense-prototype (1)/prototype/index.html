A signal that reads the road.

This is the working core of SignalSense, running in your browser. A YOLOv8 model watches recorded footage from a real junction camera, counts the vehicles waiting on each approach, scores them in PCU and plans each green from that score. Load your own video or your own trained model and it runs on that instead.

    What is real here

    The detections. Every box comes from YOLOv8n running on the video frames in your browser through ONNX Runtime WebAssembly, so the counts, the PCU score and the green times all follow what the model sees. Nothing is scripted.

    PCU weights follow IRC 106-1990: two-wheeler 0.5, bicycle 0.4, car 1.0, auto-rickshaw 1.2, bus and truck 2.2.

    What is not, yet

    The signals are virtual. The footage is recorded, so its real traffic lights keep their own plan and cars don't react to our decisions. That is shadow mode, step 2 of our pilot plan. The stock model knows COCO classes only: auto-rickshaws and ambulances need our custom Indian model, which this page loads through "Load my model".
